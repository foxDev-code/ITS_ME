Challenges

Due to the inherent complex system of the platform, we needed to ensure that all vital information on the page is clear, and all tasks are easily learnable and understandable.

To achieve this, we have created a simple design system that will enforce all elements to look and operate the same way. With this in place, we create a sense of familiarity and learnability with the user once they have used the platform a few times.

My role • Product Designer

  • Lead the designs from the user experience to the branding of the app.

  • Collaborate with the engineer to make sure it works in our in-house app development tool, Wanilla

  • Lead research efforts to ensure the app is flexible with different languages

  • Keep all resources and cost to a minimum

Jessica Chapplow approached me with an idea to help remove queues in humanitarian aid efforts (hence the name). The concept is to utilize Wanilla, a no-code platform being developed in the background, to create a mobile app that connects people in need with the appropriate aid.


Our main challenge is to filter out the noise from conversations and discussions that involve coordinating different aid efforts. Therefore, we are focusing on providing clear information and structured conversations.
